Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 27, 2025

Overview

Solana Portfolio ("Saul", "we", "our") is a privacy-focused portfolio tracker for Solana wallets. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data. Specifically:

  • No account required - You don't need to sign up or provide any personal information
  • No tracking - We don't use analytics, cookies, or tracking pixels
  • No data sharing - We never sell or share your data with third parties

Wallet Information

When you enter a wallet address:

  • The address is only used to fetch publicly available blockchain data
  • Wallet addresses are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) for convenience
  • You can clear this data at any time by disconnecting or clearing your browser data
  • We do not store wallet addresses on our servers beyond temporary caching for performance

Payment Information

If you choose to upgrade to Pro:

  • Payments are made directly on the Solana blockchain
  • We only store your wallet address and payment transaction signature
  • We do not collect credit card or banking information

Local Storage

We store the following in your browser's localStorage:

  • Wallet addresses you've added
  • Your portfolio labels/groups
  • Connected wallet information
  • UI preferences

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use features that require server communication.

Security

  • All connections use HTTPS encryption
  • We never ask for or have access to your private keys
  • Wallet connections use standard Solana wallet adapter protocols

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, please reach out via our website at saul.run.
